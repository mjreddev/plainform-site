Photo proof for contractors.

Jobsite photos stamped with the right time and place.

A fast camera that stamps every photo with the date, time and GPS location, files it under the right project, job and album, and turns it into a PDF photo report your client or insurer can read. It works with no signal, in English and Spanish.

Features

Everything a job needs on the phone

A fast camera

Shoot, and the photo is filed. Sharp Mode fixes focus far away for roofs and exteriors, so the shutter fires the instant you tap.

Stamps you can trust

Date, time with its own time zone, GPS and address on every photo. If the phone's clock looks wrong, the stamp says so. Imported photos are labelled as imported.

Project, job, album

Organize by project, job and album, with tags and before/after pairs. Search every photo by name, note, tag, job or address.

Notes and voice dictation

Add notes to any photo, or dictate them. Speech is recognized on the phone; the audio is never sent anywhere.

Markup and redaction

Arrows, boxes, text and measurements. Blur or black out faces, plates and addresses before a photo goes in a report.

Checklists PRO

Inspection checklists from your own templates, with photos for each item.

PDF photo reports

Cover page, photo grid, the time and GPS for every photo, notes and checklists. Share by email or any app.

Works offline

Basements, rural sites, new builds with no coverage: photos are stamped the moment you take them, and addresses fill in when the phone is back online.

English and Spanish

The whole app works in English and Spanish. With Pro, Translate with Google shows names, notes and checklists in the other language, on the phone.

FAQ

Questions

Is my data private?

Without an account, SiteProof collects nothing: your photos stay on your phone and leave it only when you share them. With SiteProof Team, your company's projects are stored in the cloud so the crew and the office can work on them, and the company owns that data. We do not sell data or use it for advertising. The details are in the SiteProof privacy policy.

Do my crew need to pay?

Paid seats are owners, admins and members. Viewers are free, and so are clients who use the client portal.

Can I cancel SiteProof Team?

Yes, at any time from the billing page on the team website. Your company keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Taking, stamping and organizing photos, markup and PDF reports all work with no signal. Addresses fill in, and Team projects sync, when the phone is back online.

Do I need SiteProof Team to use the app?

No. SiteProof works on its own with no account. SiteProof Team is optional, for companies that want to share projects between phones, the office and clients.
