Features

Rules for every kind of caller

Rules for numbers, area codes, countries, and cities

Block or silence one number, a prefix, an area code or a whole state, a country, or a city's telephone area. Allow rules and Allowed numbers keep the calls you want.

Contact, group, and name rules

Follow a person across all their numbers, a whole contacts group such as Family, or every contact whose name contains a word. Caller ID name rules (Pro) hang up on other callers by the name your Phone app shows, after a brief ring.

Voice commands with “Did you mean…?” PRO

Say “block area code 307” or “silence my Work group”. If CallRules corrected a word, it asks first, and nothing changes until you tap Confirm.

Rule check

Finds block rules that allowed numbers get past, rules that never apply, duplicates, and overlaps, and offers a fix for each.

Call history with names

See each blocked call and the reason, with the caller's name when it's a contact or a call CallRules hung up on. Allow a number or block a recent caller with one tap.

Schedules and saved places PRO

Apply a rule only during work hours, only on some days, or only at a place such as Office. Name places once and use them by voice.

Outgoing calls PRO

Block outgoing calls to numbers you choose, or ask for a PIN or a long press before a call goes out. Works with your normal Phone app.

Private by design

Rules, lists, and call history stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no cloud spam lookup.

Carrier verification

Block calls that fail the STIR/SHAKEN check your carrier attaches, or go Strict and block unverified calls too.

Hang up on spam-labeled calls PRO

When your Phone app labels a call “Spam Risk” or “Scam Likely”, CallRules hangs up after a brief ring and blocks the number, so it doesn't ring again. Optional switches cover labels some carriers add, such as Telemarketer or Survey, and Caller ID name rules hang up on names you choose. Your contacts are never hung up on.

Silence texts from blocked senders PRO

Hide the notifications of texts from senders you block, and of likely scams, and mark them read. Texts stay in your messaging app: CallRules never deletes them and uses no SMS permissions.

How it decides

Predictable, in a fixed order

Each incoming call goes through the same steps, and the first step that decides the call wins.

  1. Block rules with “Override allow rules” on block the call, even for contacts and allowed numbers.
  2. Allowed numbers, contacts, and allow rules let the call ring.
  3. Other block rules and blocked numbers reject or silence it.
  4. Carrier verification, unknown numbers, and quiet hours block what's left, if you turn them on.
  5. Anything else rings. If CallRules can't decide within about 3.5 seconds, the call rings too.

Hidden callers follow their own Private rules. With Pro, a call that rings can still be hung up on after a brief ring when your Phone app labels it as spam; contacts, allowed numbers, and allow rules never are. See every step in the guide.

Privacy

Your call data stays on your phone

CallRules checks every call on your device. Your rules, lists, contacts, and call history are not uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

The network is used only for Google Play Billing, address searches you run, Google Sheets import, links you open, and things you choose to share. Voice commands use Android's on-device speech recognizer. If you turn on hanging up on labeled calls, CallRules reads your Phone app's incoming-call notification on the phone, only to decide; it isn't sent anywhere.

FAQ

Questions

Does CallRules replace my Phone app?

No. CallRules works as Android's Caller ID & spam app alongside your normal Phone app. It isn't a dialer.

Can an area-code or country rule tell where a caller is?

No. Area-code, country, and city rules match the start of the phone number, not the caller's location. Location rules use where your phone is.

Is there a spam database?

No. There's no spam database or cloud spam lookup. You decide what to block, and carrier verification uses the check your carrier already runs. With Pro, CallRules can also hang up on calls your carrier or Phone app labels as spam, such as “Spam Risk” or “Scam Likely”: the phone rings briefly, then CallRules hangs up and blocks the number.

Why did a contact still get through?

Allowed numbers, contacts, and allow rules come before ordinary block rules. Turn on “Override allow rules” on the block rule, and use Rule check to find rules that are bypassed.

Does it work with text messages?

It can silence them. With Silence texts (Pro), CallRules hides the notification of a text from a sender you block and marks it read; the text stays in your messaging app. It never deletes texts, and it doesn't ask for SMS permissions.

Is Lifetime Pro a subscription?

No. It's a single purchase through Google Play, and you can restore it on a new phone with the same Google account.
