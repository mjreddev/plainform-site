Features

Rules for every kind of caller

Rules for numbers, area codes, countries, and cities

Block or silence one number, a prefix, an area code or a whole state, a country, or a city's telephone area. Allow rules and a whitelist keep the calls you want.

Contact, group, and caller-name rules

Follow a person across all their numbers, a whole contacts group such as Family, or a caller-ID name such as “SCAM LIKELY”.

Voice commands with “Did you mean…?” PRO

Say “block area code 307” or “silence my Work group”. If CallRules corrected a word, it asks first, and nothing changes until you tap Confirm.

Rule check

Finds block rules that allowed numbers get past, rules that never apply, duplicates, and overlaps, and offers a fix for each.

Call history with names

See each blocked call with the caller's name and the reason. Allow a number or block a recent caller with one tap.

Schedules and saved places PRO

Apply a rule only during work hours, only on some days, or only at a place such as Office. Name places once and use them by voice.

Outgoing protection PRO

Block outgoing calls to numbers you choose, or ask for a PIN or a long press before a call goes out. Works with your normal Phone app.

Private by design

Rules, lists, and call history stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no cloud spam lookup.

Carrier verification

Block calls that fail the STIR/SHAKEN check your carrier attaches, or go Strict and block unverified calls too.

How it decides

Predictable, in a fixed order

Each incoming call goes through the same steps, and the first step that decides the call wins.

  1. Block rules with “Override allow rules” on block the call, even for contacts and allowed numbers.
  2. Your whitelist, contacts, and allow rules let the call ring.
  3. Other block rules and blocked numbers reject or silence it.
  4. Carrier verification, unknown numbers, and quiet hours block what's left, if you turn them on.
  5. Anything else rings. If CallRules can't decide within about 3.5 seconds, the call rings too.

Hidden callers follow their own Private rules. See every step in the guide.

Privacy

Your call data stays on your phone

CallRules checks every call on your device. Your rules, lists, contacts, and call history are not uploaded. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

The network is used only for Google Play Billing, address searches you run, Google Sheets import, links you open, and things you choose to share. Voice commands use Android's on-device speech recognizer.

FAQ

Questions

Does CallRules replace my Phone app?

No. CallRules works as Android's Caller ID & spam app alongside your normal Phone app. It isn't a dialer.

Can an area-code or country rule tell where a caller is?

No. Area-code, country, and city rules match the start of the phone number, not the caller's location. Location rules use where your phone is.

Is there a spam database?

There's no cloud spam lookup. You decide what to block, and carrier verification uses the check your carrier already runs.

Why did a contact still get through?

Your whitelist, contacts, and allow rules come before ordinary block rules. Turn on “Override allow rules” on the block rule, and use Rule check to find rules that are bypassed.

Does it block text messages?

No. CallRules works with phone calls only.

Is Lifetime Pro a subscription?

No. It's a single purchase through Google Play, and you can restore it on a new phone with the same Google account.
