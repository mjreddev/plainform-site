Privacy Policy

Plainform · MJRed Dev LLC · Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version. Our apps do not sell personal information, serve advertising, or include analytics or tracking SDKs. Most processing happens on your device. Information leaves the device only when needed for a service you request or when you choose to export, share, or send it, as described below.

The rest of this page explains, per app, exactly what data is handled and why.

1. Who we are

Plainform is the trading name of MJRed Dev LLC ("we", "us"), which publishes Android applications through Google Play. This policy applies to all applications we publish. You can reach us at mjred.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

Our apps have no user accounts or login and do not automatically upload app content or diagnostics to us. We cannot see information processed only on your device. We receive information only if you deliberately send it to us, such as by emailing feedback or a problem report.

We do not include advertising networks, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, or any other third-party tracking libraries in our applications.

3. Data the apps process on your device

Our apps need access to certain information in order to function. That information is primarily processed locally. Any user-requested transfer to another service or app is described in section 5.

CallRules

  • Incoming-call information and contacts — to identify callers and evaluate your blocking and allow rules. This processing happens on the device. CallRules does not handle SMS or MMS. Cloud spam lookup is disabled for this release, and incoming phone numbers are not automatically sent to a cloud spam service.
  • Location — optional approximate or precise location is used only to evaluate location-constrained rules. If you enable those rules, background location may be needed so they can work when the app is not open. Saved locations remain on the device unless you include them in a configuration export.
  • Microphone — optional, used only when you start a voice command. On supported devices CallRules uses Android's on-device speech recognizer and does not silently substitute online recognition. Speech transcripts are not stored in diagnostics.
  • Blocked numbers, whitelist entries, call-blocking rules, saved locations, settings, and limited call history may be stored in the app's private storage on the device.
  • Diagnostics — attaching a report requires your explicit choice. A report may include structured event identifiers, timestamps, screening outcomes and duration, permission or role status, location availability, purchase error codes, import failure codes, and sanitized crash stack frames. They exclude phone numbers, contact names, rule names and patterns, coordinates, speech transcripts, purchase tokens, and raw exception messages. Reports describe CallRules's decisions; they cannot establish what a carrier or another phone component subsequently did.
  • Exports and imports — configuration exports are created only when you request them and may include enabled and disabled rules, blocked numbers, whitelist entries, saved locations, and selected settings. They exclude purchase entitlements, Android permission grants, call history, and diagnostic history. Google Sheets imports occur only when you supply a published sheet URL.

File Zone

  • Storage access — to browse, search, and manage your files, which is the app's function.
  • Network credentials — if you connect to your own network shares (SMB, FTP, SFTP, WebDAV), credentials are stored encrypted on the device using the Android Keystore, and are sent only to the server you configured.
  • File contents are never transmitted to us.

SiteProof

  • Camera — to take jobsite photos.
  • Location (while in use) — to record where a photo was taken and to stamp it onto the image. We do not request background location access.
  • Microphone — optional, only when you record a voice note.
  • Photos, reports, and job records are stored on your device. If you turn on backup, files are copied to a folder you choose; we have no access to it.
  • Reverse geocoding (turning coordinates into a street address) uses the Android system service, which may contact Google. See section 5.

4. Purchases

Paid features are sold as one-time in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google; we never see or store your payment details. CallRules stores only the resulting ownership state for offline access and excludes purchase tokens from diagnostics and configuration exports.

5. Third-party services

We do not embed third-party trackers. Our apps use or hand information to platform and third-party services only to complete actions you request:

  • Google Play Billing — to process in-app purchases and verify entitlements.
  • Android system geocoding (SiteProof only) — converts coordinates into a readable address. This request is made by the Android operating system and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The feature degrades gracefully when offline: the app records coordinates only.
  • Android system geocoding (CallRules, when requested) — may send the address query to the device's configured geocoder to return coordinates.
  • Google Sheets import (CallRules, when requested) — retrieves the published sheet URL you provide. The service receives the network request needed to return that sheet.
  • Maps, email, and sharing apps (CallRules, when you choose them) — receive the location, feedback, problem report, diagnostic attachment, or exported file you direct CallRules to hand off. You control whether to complete sending in the selected app. Opening another app does not by itself mean a report was delivered.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not automatically upload call data or diagnostics. Information is disclosed to a platform service or destination app only when needed for an action you request, as described in section 5.

7. Data retention and deletion

App-private data remains on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app according to Android behavior. Where an app offers an export or backup feature, copies you create are under your control and must be deleted through the app or location where you saved them.

While CallRules is running, it opportunistically removes diagnostic records older than seven days and generated sharing files older than 24 hours, and enforces a 1 MiB diagnostic-log limit when recording. Files may remain longer while CallRules is inactive. You can clear diagnostics and generated sharing files in the app at any time.

We do not maintain an account or app-content database about you. If you send us an email, you may contact us to request deletion of that correspondence.

8. Children

Our apps are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Security

App data is stored in private application storage protected by Android's sandbox. Network credentials, where applicable, are encrypted using the Android Keystore. Network connections our apps make on your behalf use encrypted protocols where the destination server supports them.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. Most information described here remains under your control on your own device. For information you deliberately send us, contact us using the address below to exercise any applicable rights.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes affecting how an app handles data will also be noted in that app's release notes.
