YouTube Uploader Privacy Policy

Plainform · MJRed Dev LLC · Effective 6 October 2026

The short version. The Plainform YouTube uploader is an internal script that we run on our own computer to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel. Nobody outside Plainform uses it, it accesses no one else's YouTube account or data, and it shares nothing with anyone.

1. Who we are

Plainform is the trading name of MJRed Dev LLC ("we", "us"). You can reach us at mjred.dev@gmail.com. This policy covers only the YouTube uploader tool. Our Android apps have their own policies, linked from privacy.html, and do not use YouTube API Services.

2. What the tool is

The uploader is a command-line program that uses YouTube API Services to upload product videos and app-review walkthroughs to the Plainform YouTube channel, and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, captions, and thumbnails. It is used only by Plainform staff. It has no public interface and is not offered to other users.

3. Data it accesses and stores

  • Our authorization: when we sign in with Google, the tool receives an access token for our own YouTube channel. The token is stored only on our own computer.
  • Our uploads: the tool keeps a local record of the IDs and links of the videos it uploads, so it does not upload the same video twice.
  • It does not access, collect, or store any other user's YouTube data, and it does not read comments, analytics, or other channels.

4. Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data the tool handles. Video files and their text go only to YouTube, as part of publishing our own videos.

5. Retention and deletion

The access token and upload record stay on our computer until we delete them. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's Third-party apps & services page, which stops the tool from using YouTube API Services.

6. YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
